About Me

I am Brent Delgado, a toy collector and enthusiast. I am only 20 years old, but my love for collecting rare and unique toy pieces has been a passion of mine since I was a little kid.

I want to share my collection with the public to showcase the beauty, art, and story behind each toy. I hope to inspire others to start their own toy collections or find a hobby in my shop.

A toy is truly a man's best friend. That's the message I want to spread around the globe.
